# Snake

![Snake](../../images/Ableton%20Extension%20-%20Snake.png)

[⬇️ Download](../../download/snake.ablx)

Play Snake inside Ableton Live and turn your gameplay into a MIDI clip.

## Features

- **Playable game** — Snake embedded in a dialog, controlled by arrow keys or WASD
- **Pause support** — Press Space to pause mid-game
- **Auto MIDI generation** — Every directional move creates a note mapped to a pitch
- **Instant placement** — When the game ends, notes are placed in a MIDI clip on your track

## How to use

1. Right-click any **MIDI Track** and choose **🐍 Play Snake**.
2. Use **arrow keys** or **WASD** to steer. Press **Space** to pause.
3. Eat food to grow. Avoid walls and yourself.
4. When the game ends, a MIDI clip is created on your track — each directional change maps to a different pitch.
